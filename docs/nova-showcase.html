Full Feature Set

Everything Nova Manages

Four capability areas covering the entire email lifecycle — from template creation to meeting conversion.

Command Center

Real-time email analytics and activity monitoring

6 views
KPI Dashboard
6 metrics: Emails Sent, Open Rate, Reply Rate, Meetings Booked, Bounce Rate, and Queue Depth.
Email Volume Chart
3-series area chart: sent, opened, replied over time with period selector.
Engagement Funnel
Horizontal stacked bars: Sent → Delivered → Opened → Clicked → Replied → Meetings. Each with percentage and count.
Recent Activity Feed
Last 10 emails with status badges: delivered, opened, clicked, replied, bounced, failed, sending.
Top Performers
Emails with highest open counts. See which subjects and personalization strategies actually work.
Peak Hours Heatmap
7-day × 3 time-block grid (Morning, Afternoon, Evening) showing send intensity patterns.

Template & Sequence Engine

Build, organize, and automate multi-step email flows

5 capabilities
Template Editor
Build email templates with {{variable}} placeholders. Subject line, HTML body, plain text fallback. Track usage count and performance.
5 Template Categories
Organized: outreach, follow-up, nurture, re-engagement, meeting-request. Filter and search across your library.
Sequence Builder
Multi-step email sequences with delays and conditions. Step 1: intro (Day 0) → Step 2: follow-up (Day 3, if no reply) → Step 3: breakup (Day 7).
Variable Substitution
{{name}}, {{company}}, {{role}} — extracted automatically from templates, filled per-lead at send time.
Performance Tracking
Per-template avg_open_rate and avg_reply_rate. Know exactly which templates convert.

Campaign Management

Launch, control, and measure email campaigns

5 capabilities
3-Step Campaign Wizard
Setup (name, template, leads) → Send Settings (limits, window, timezone) → Review & Launch.
Daily Send Limits
Configurable per campaign. Default: 50 emails/day. Prevents spam flags and maintains deliverability.
Time-Window Scheduling
Send only during business hours. Default: 09:00-17:00 in your timezone. Customizable per campaign.
Status Tracking
Campaign lifecycle: draft → active → paused → completed. Pause mid-campaign and resume anytime.
Campaign Analytics
Per-campaign: total leads, emails sent, delivered, opened, clicked, replied, bounced, meetings booked, total cost.

Engagement Tracking

Track every open, click, reply, and conversion

6 capabilities
Open Tracking
First open timestamp, total open count. Know exactly when and how many times each email is read.
Click Tracking
Click timestamp and click count. Track which links in your emails drive action.
Reply Detection
Reply timestamp with snippet preview. See the first line of every reply without leaving the dashboard.
Bounce Handling
Bounce type (hard/soft), timestamp. Automatically flags problem addresses.
Meeting Detection
Boolean flag when reply contains calendar/meeting links. Tracks conversion from email to meeting.
8-Status Lifecycle
Every email moves through: queued → sending → delivered → opened → clicked → replied (or bounced/failed). Full timestamp trail.
