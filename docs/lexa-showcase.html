Full Feature Set

Everything Lexa Tracks

Three systems working together — your command center, campaign engine, and analytics intelligence.

Command Center

Real-time dashboard with KPIs, charts, and live activity

6 views
KPI Dashboard
6 metrics at a glance: Total Calls, Minutes, Avg Duration, Answer Rate, Avg Latency, and Total Cost.
Call Volume Chart
Area chart with period selector (Today, 7 Days, 30 Days, 90 Days, All Time). See calling patterns over time.
Call Outcomes
Pie chart showing distribution: completed, failed, voicemail, no-answer. Know exactly where calls end up.
Recent Activity Feed
Last 10 calls with color-coded badges for inbound, outbound, and campaign calls. Live updates via Supabase realtime.
Cost Analysis
Stacked bar chart breaking down costs: STT (20%), TTS (25%), LLM (40%), Millis credits (15%).
Status Bar
Live indicator with credit balance and assigned phone number. Red alert when credits drop below 100.

Campaign Engine

Build, launch, and track outbound calling campaigns

5 capabilities
Campaign Builder
Create outbound campaigns with AI prompts, call delays, and retry logic. Assign a lead list and launch.
Lead Management
Upload contacts with custom fields. Track each lead: pending, calling, completed, failed, skipped.
Batch Processing
Dials through 50 leads per batch with configurable delays between calls. Respects rate limits.
Retry Logic
Failed calls get retried automatically. Configurable max retries with attempt tracking.
Progress Tracking
Real-time campaign stats: calls made, answered, voicemail, failed, avg duration, total cost.

Analytics & Intelligence

Sentiment, trends, and cost intelligence from every call

4 capabilities
Sentiment Analysis
Every call gets a sentiment score: positive, negative, or neutral. Keyword-based analysis on the full transcript.
Duration Trends
Track average call duration over time. Spot patterns in how long conversations last.
Answer Rate Tracking
Percentage of calls that connect vs. voicemail, failed, or no-answer. Campaign-level and overall.
Cost Breakdown
Per-call cost tracking: STT, TTS, LLM, and Millis platform credits. See exactly where money goes.
Under the Hood

4 Production Tables

Purpose-built schema for call tracking, campaigns, leads, and pre-aggregated daily metrics.

lexa_calls
21 columns — Full call lifecycle with transcript, cost, metrics, and VoIP data
lexa_campaigns
16 columns — Campaign config with AI prompts, delays, retry settings, and progress stats
lexa_leads
17 columns — Per-lead tracking: status, call result, sentiment, attempts, custom fields
lexa_daily_metrics
12 columns — Pre-aggregated daily stats for fast dashboard rendering
Edge Functions

3 Serverless Functions

Deployed to Supabase Edge Functions. Each handles a distinct part of the call lifecycle.

lexa-webhook lexa-webhook
Receives call data from Millis AI. Parses transcripts, calculates costs, upserts call records, updates daily metrics.
Webhook
lexa-campaign-runner lexa-campaign-runner
Batch-dials lead lists. Fetches pending leads, calls Millis API, updates statuses, tracks progress.
Webhook
millis-proxy millis-proxy
API bridge to Millis AI platform. Creates agents, checks credit balances, manages phone numbers.
REST
A fourth function, lexa-precall, validates lead data before campaign launch — checking phone formats, deduplicating numbers, and flagging invalid entries.
