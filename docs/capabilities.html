Core Capabilities

Everything your agent needs to be visible, trackable, and autonomous.

📋

Kanban Task Board

5-column board (To Do, Doing, Needs Input, Canceled, Done) with real-time updates. Agents create, move, and complete tasks. Subtasks for granular tracking.

Core
📝

AI Activity Log

Agents journal every decision, observation, and discovery. Categorized entries (general, observation, reminder, FYI) with unread badges and search.

Core
❓

Questions & Approvals

Async Q&A between you and your agent. When the agent is stuck, it asks through the dashboard. You answer on your schedule. Full decision history.

Core
📈

Insights & Analytics

Agents push performance cards, alerts, summaries, and suggestions. Real-time analytics that appear on your dashboard as work completes.

Core
📄

HTML Reports

Formatted, styled reports generated by your agent. Employee reports, insight summaries, competitive analysis. Stored and searchable.

Core
🎯

Agent Status Ring

Real-time presence indicator. Green = working, Yellow = waiting, Red = blocked, Gray = offline. See exactly what your agent is doing at a glance.

Core

Automation Engine

Scheduled workflows that run without you touching anything.

⏰

Scheduled Automations

Define cron-based automations that run via pg_cron. Morning digests, evening reports, competitor intel, meeting prep. Full execution tracking.

Automation
🧠

Sherlock Brain

Self-improving AI that analyzes past runs, computes health scores, auto-tunes settings, and discovers stale tasks. Runs every 4 hours automatically.

Automation
📨

Multi-Channel Delivery

Automation results delivered via dashboard, AgentMail, Resend email, Telegram, or Discord. Configure channels per automation.

Automation
🔍

AI Web Research

Browser research with configurable AI providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Kimi, Groq). Research any URL or topic. Results auto-stored in your dashboard.

Automation
📅

Calendar Integration

Sync with Google Calendar via Make.com. Search upcoming events, create new ones. Calendar data flows into morning digests and meeting prep.

Automation
📊

Competitor Intelligence

Track YouTube channels, detect viral content, analyze competitor strategies. Full HTML reports with engagement metrics and trend alerts.
